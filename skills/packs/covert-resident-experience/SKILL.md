# Covert Resident experience

## Scope/contracts
Read docs/design/workstation/CIPHER_AND_BUDDY_CONTRACT.md and scoped acceptance rows. Reuse Resident binding, Context Control, Orchestrator, Authority, Admission and evidence owners. No second assistant/memory/executor.

## Procedure
1. Pin Resident binding/project, canonical fact sources and available qualified adapters.
2. Rehost compact Standard status/invoked window; preserve deterministic controls when AI fails.
3. Separate discover/observe/read/remember/propose/delegate/request-execute/verify. Terminal existence is not scrollback permission; credentials excluded.
4. Keep Resident/personality/voice/chassis/animation/idle independent. Opt-in preferences never grant capabilities.
5. Map binding/capture/output/task/attention/cosmetic dimensions independently.
6. Bounded passive built-in assets/static fallback; selected pack lazy cached.
7. Safe deterministic homes/exclusions; no place means hidden body with ordinary Cipher status.
8. Hidden/reduced motion suspend animation; coalesce meaningful events, no telemetry chatter.

## Capture/vision

Follow WORKSTATION_SECURITY_CONTRACT.md and its closure matrix in the same contract directory. Resident needs a unique trusted-enrolled principal, not operator/worker/app identity. Resolve current context/grants through owners at use time. Treat source/media/model instructions as data, preserve continuity provenance and never store plaintext secrets. Cipher's laptop is scoped records, not a privileged machine. Integrity and panic are deterministic, independent of inference.
Qualify explicit STT/TTS/vision routes; browser speech not assumed local. Preview scope/egress; capture/routing/retention separate.

Independent Stop Listening/Stop Speaking/Mute Output/Buddy Off. Mute does not stop capture. Hide cannot hide capture indicators. Buddy Off stops optional capture/output/presence, not legitimate tasks. Lock/sleep/restart stop optional media; restore preferences not live mic. No voice/model selfapproval bypassing Authority.

Structured facts before screenshots; purposeful selected-image route, no ambient surveillance/silent fallback.

## Examples/anti-patterns
Rook + Developers Special and Mutt + Calm Professional preserve ResidentID/grants/context/tasks. Tiny laptop typing is cosmetic, not WORKING. Lens green is not recording; cheerful pose is not VERIFIED. No specialist started for idle clips or all packs decoded on boot.

## Verification/recovery
Prove profile/chassis swaps preserve identity/scope; actual work/capture truth; no obstruction; hidden/reduced motion; denied/device-lost routes and immediate stop; independent tools during AI outage. Test exact Resident artifact/prompt budget; measure same-host Buddy on/off overhead.

Lost owner truth displays unknown/degraded and discards stale capture claims; stop controls remain reachable. Never replay approvals or silently select cloud.
