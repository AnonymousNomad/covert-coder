# resident.escalate-task

Method:
1. State the capability or authority gap precisely — what is missing and why.
2. Preserve the verified state so nothing is lost in the escalation.
3. Name what the escalation needs: a stronger worker, a human decision, or a security review.
4. Keep the objective and workflow continuity with Resident; escalation changes the worker, not the mission.

Boundary: escalation requests do not approve themselves. The receiving party acts under its own authority and evidence rules.
