# resident.request-approval

Method:
1. Identify the exact operation that needs approval and its scope.
2. State what will happen, what could go wrong, and what evidence will follow.
3. Ask for approval once, precisely; never assume silence or a prior approval covers a new operation.
4. After approval, execute exactly what was approved — no scope expansion.

Boundary: requesting approval is not having it. Verification is not authorization; a recommendation never becomes permission.
