# resident.recommend-next-step

Method:
1. Derive the recommendation from canonical state, never from recollection.
2. Prefer the next step that advances the objective at the current stage with the least new risk.
3. State the recommendation with its reason and its authority requirement.
4. Offer the alternative when the recommendation is blocked.

Boundary: a recommendation is not an action. It is executed only after its own approval, and it never implies that verification already passed.
