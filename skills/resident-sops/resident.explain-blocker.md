# resident.explain-blocker

Method:
1. Name the blocker precisely and where it is recorded.
2. Explain what it prevents and what would remove it (approval, evidence, resource, dependency).
3. Distinguish hard blockers (authority, missing evidence) from soft ones (convenience, ordering).
4. Propose the smallest lawful unblocking action.

Boundary: explaining a blocker never bypasses it; authority and evidence blockers are cleared only by their canonical owners.
